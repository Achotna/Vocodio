What is Vocodio?

Learning a language? With Vocodio, enhance your word lists with smart audio: listen to the word, repeat and think, then hear the translation.

No more struggling to find time to review vocabulary: every moment becomes a learning opportunity!

A method that really helps you remember

Vocodio is an innovative solution that turns a simple list into an immersive learning experience. Thanks to built-in pauses, your brain stays actively engaged: you try to guess before hearing the answer. The result is faster, longer-lasting, and fully customizable memorization.
